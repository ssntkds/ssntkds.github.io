This is an unofficial experimental pet project aimed at simplifying the railway user experience by trying to convey as much information about railways as possible.

The map is designed around a 30-degree grid with smooth, flowing geometry inspired by Stadler train design.

It integrates bus connections to airports and ports, highlights accessibility for passengers with reduced mobility, and clearly marks stations served by EC, IC, and ICS services.

The visualization approach focuses on clarity and ease of navigation while maintaining aesthetic appeal.
